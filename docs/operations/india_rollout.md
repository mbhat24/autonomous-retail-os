# India Rollout Plan

## Beachhead Market

Start with controlled environments:

- Apartment society stores
- Office pantry stores
- Hostels
- Campuses
- Gated community mini marts
- Premium fruit and vegetable stores

These locations have known customers, lower theft risk, and easier camera setup.

## Expansion Sequence

1. Autonomous mini markets in controlled environments
2. Smart kirana stores with camera-assisted checkout
3. Vegetable/fruit shops with smart scale automation
4. Franchise micro-stores
5. ONDC-connected local commerce
6. Large supermarkets

## Commercial Model

### Tier 1

- Setup: ₹0-₹5,000
- SaaS: ₹99-₹299/month

### Tier 2

- Setup: ₹25,000-₹75,000
- SaaS: ₹1,999-₹5,999/month

### Tier 3

- Setup: ₹2 lakh-₹15 lakh
- SaaS + transaction fee + monitoring fee

## India-Specific Requirements

- UPI-first payment
- WhatsApp notifications
- Local language UI
- Offline-first operation
- GST-ready reports
- Khata/credit support for assisted stores
- Loose item support for vegetables and fruits
- Mandi/supplier pricing support
- Privacy notices and consent management

## Operational Teams Needed

- Hardware installation team
- Camera calibration team
- Remote monitoring team
- Merchant onboarding team
- Payment integration support
- Supplier partnership team
- Field maintenance partners
